### About

I'm an **Applied AI Engineer** building reliable AI agents, developer tools, and full-stack software for real operational problems.

My work combines **Python and TypeScript engineering with product thinking and commercial experience**. I build internal software and decision-support workflows, and independently develop agent infrastructure focused on execution, evaluation, and failure recovery.

I care about the entire engineering process: understanding the problem, implementing the system, testing its behavior, and being clear about what the evidence does—and doesn't—prove.

**Build the system. Test the behavior. Show the evidence.**
